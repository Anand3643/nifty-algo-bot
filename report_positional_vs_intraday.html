⚠️ The Brutal Reality: What Happens if You Hold Positional Without Intraday Square-off?

1. Current Weekly Options Get Destroyed Overnight

Holding Current Weekly options overnight causes massive performance collapse (Loss of Rs 92,301.38). Why? Overnight Theta decay evaporates 20–35 points every single night without any market movement. Furthermore, when NIFTY gaps against the position at 09:15 IST, the 50 pt stop loss gets completely gapped over, triggering deep slippage losses.

2. Intraday Trading Wins Because of Overnight Immunity

Intraday trading at 15:10 IST has zero overnight risk. By closing before the bell, you sleep in cash with 100% peace of mind, completely immune to US market crashes, Middle East geopolitical shocks, or GIFT Nifty morning gaps.

3. If You MUST Trade Positional: Use Next Week or Monthly Only!

If positional holding is strictly required, NEVER carry Current Weekly contracts. Carry Next Weekly (7–10 DTE) or Monthly contracts (15–30 DTE) where daily theta decay is slow and linear. However, intraday execution remains 2.5x more profitable with half the drawdown.
