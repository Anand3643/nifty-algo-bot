2. How Did the SEBI Reform (Sep 1, 2025) Impact Expiries?

The shift from Thursday to Tuesday expiries on September 1, 2025 changed the weekly theta decay curve. Post-reform Tuesday expiries now have only 1 trading day (Monday) between the weekend and expiry day, concentrating theta decay heavily into Monday afternoon and Tuesday morning. Rolling to Next Week on Tuesdays is even more effective post-reform to avoid Tuesday morning theta erosion.
