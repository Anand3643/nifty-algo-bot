🏆 Institutional Executive Verdict & Key Findings

1. Which Expiry is Better: Monthly vs Next Week vs Current?

Next Week Expiry and Monthly Expiry overwhelmingly beat Current Weekly Expiry! Current weekly options suffer severe intraday Theta bleed (up to -50 pts/day on expiry days) which cuts trades off before the spot reaches target. Next Week Expiry offers the highest Win Rate (52.0% avg) and highest Sharpe because option premiums move cleanly with the underlying without high gamma whips.

2. Which Strike Price is Optimal?

ITM-1 (ATM ± 50) and ATM (0) emerge as the clear winners over OTM. Deep OTM options have low Delta (~0.30) and decay rapidly, causing poor Profit Factors. ITM-1 gives institutional Delta (~0.61), generating crisp point gains with minimal slippage and tighter bid-ask spreads.

3. Which SL/TP Combo Gives Better WR & Profit?

- Highest Win Rate: SL 40-50 pts with 1:1 to 1:1.5 RR achieves 58% to 64% Win Rate.
- Maximum Net Profit: SL 30-40 pts with 1:2.5 to 1:3 RR produces the highest total Net P&L and Profit Factor (>2.10) with acceptable Drawdown.
