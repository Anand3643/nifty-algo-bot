4 Actionable Rules to Maximize Profit & Suppress Drawdown
Implementation guidelines derived directly from the backtest results.
1

Prune CPR Chop & Trade Pure Outer Levels

Never take trades inside the Central Pivot Range (CPR). 100% of the CPR trades were false breakouts. Restrict entries strictly to R1, R2, S1, and S2 where institutional liquidity resides.

2

Widen Option SL to 40 Pts (Eliminate Noise Stopouts)

A 30-point SL on ATM options is too tight for NIFTY's intraday wicks, leading to 18 stop-outs. A 40-point SL with a 120-point Target (1:3 RR) reduced stop-outs from 18 to just 5 while doubling net profits.

3

Require 35% Candlestick Wick Confirmation

For bouncebacks, insist on an upper or lower wick equal to at least 35% of the total 5-minute candle range. This confirms institutional price rejection rather than just a weak pause.

4

Permit Max 2 Waves Per Day (Morning + Afternoon)

Allowing a second trade when an afternoon trend develops increases total opportunities to 58 trades without overtrading, capturing both morning expansion and afternoon reversals.
