Solution 1: Gap Retest & Polarity Bounce / Rejection (61.0% Win Rate | +₹53,434 PnL)

Mechanism: On a Gap-Up > R1, the old resistance level (R1) flips into new support. When price pulls back between 09:25 and 11:30 to test R1 from above and prints a lower rejection wick, buy ATM CE! On Gap-Down < S1, old support flips to new resistance; when price bounces to test S1 from below and rejects, buy ATM PE!

Solution 2: Gap-and-Go Opening Range Breakout (ORB)
