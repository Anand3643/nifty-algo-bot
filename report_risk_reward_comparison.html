Institutional Analytics: Why 1:3 Dominates on Breakout Trades

  • Breakout Momentum Expansion: Unlike fade/reversal trades (which suffer from mean reversion), when Spot breaks through S1 or R1, trapped participants liquidate and momentum accelerates. S1 Support Breakdowns achieved a 61.9% Win Rate and produced +Rs 18,452.90 Net PnL (+303.4 premium points)!
  • 1:3 vs 1:2 Comparison: On 25 pts SL, setting the target to 75 pts (1:3 RR) captured +Rs 12,594.89 compared to only +Rs 7,055.86 for 1:2 RR, an +78% increase in net profit while actually lowering Max Drawdown from 20.9% down to 17.6%!
  • S1 Support Breakdown Power: Bearish flushes below S1 are much faster and more violent than upside breakouts due to long unwinding panic in Indian index options.
